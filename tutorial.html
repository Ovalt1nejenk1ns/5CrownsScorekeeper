🃏 How to Play Five Crowns 🃏

Game Overview

Five Crowns is a rummy-style card game where players try to go out with the fewest points. The game uses a unique five-suited deck with stars, clubs, hearts, spades, and diamonds.

Game Structure

  • Rounds: The game has 11 rounds (3 through 13)
  • Cards per Round: In round 3, each player gets 3 cards; in round 4, each gets 4 cards, and so on up to 13 cards in round 13
  • Wild Cards: The wild card changes each round to match the round number (3s are wild in round 3, 4s in round 4, etc.)
  • Jokers: Jokers are ALWAYS wild in every round

How to Play a Round

  1. Each player is dealt cards equal to the round number
  2. Players take turns drawing from the deck or discard pile
  3. Try to form sets (3+ cards of same rank) and runs (3+ cards in sequence of same suit)
  4. When a player goes out (plays all their cards), others get one more turn
  5. Count remaining cards in hand as points (lower is better!)

Scoring

  • 3-9: Face value (3 = 3 points)
  • 10: 10 points
  • Jack: 11 points
  • Queen: 12 points
  • King: 13 points
  • This round's wild card: 20 points
  • Joker: 50 points

Winning

After all 11 rounds are complete, the player with the LOWEST total score wins!

Using This Scorekeeper

  1. Enter each player's name and pick the first dealer
  2. After each round, enter each player's score
  3. The app tracks totals and shows who's winning
  4. Tap any score in the round-by-round grid to fix a mistake
  5. After round 13, the winner is announced!

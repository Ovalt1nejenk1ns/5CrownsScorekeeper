    Playing a round

    1. 11 rounds. Deal 3 cards each in round 1, 4 in round 2, and so on up to 13.
    2. The wild card changes: it matches the number of cards dealt. 3s are wild in the first round, then 4s, up to Kings. Jokers are always wild.
    3. Each turn: draw from the deck or the discard pile, then discard one card.
    4. Make books and runs: books are 3+ cards of the same rank; runs are 3+ cards in a row in the same suit (five suits: stars, clubs, hearts, spades, diamonds).
    5. Going out: when your whole hand is in books and runs, lay it down. Everyone else gets one more turn, then counts what's left.
    6. The deal moves to the left each round.
